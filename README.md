Submission 

# What Counts

**What Counts** is a narrative driven maze game inspired by the theme ‘**Countdown’.** We wanted to look at the idea of a countdown differently. Instead of counting down the time we have left, we wanted to remind players to think about **what really counts**.

The game follows someone trapped in a coma. As the countdown continues, they must relive the happiest memories of their life, face the negative thoughts inside their mind, and hopefully find peace before time runs out.

## Gameplay

The game is split into three levels, each representing a different stage of the journey.

### Level 1 – Memories

Race through a maze collecting as many happy memories as possible before the doors close. Every memory you collect will help you in the next stage.

### Level 2 – The Mind

You are transported into the character's mind where you must find your way through the maze while avoiding bad thoughts that are trying to stop you. The happy memories you collected in Level 1 protect you as you make your way through.

### Level 3 – Inner Peace

If you make it through the mind, you reach a peaceful Zen garden where the journey comes to an end. Rather than beating the countdown, the game is about accepting a life filled with memories that mattered.

**Don't count down time. Remember what counts.**

Creativity - We wanted to do something different with the Countdown theme. Rather than making the timer the main challenge, we made it part of the story. The player experiences a journey through someone's mind where memories become both the objective and the way to survive.

Enjoyment - We wanted each level to feel different so players always had something new to experience. The game mixes exploration, collecting memories, avoiding danger and finally reaching a calm ending, taking players on an emotional journey rather than just another maze game.

Narrative - The story follows someone in a coma looking back on the happiest moments of their life. Those memories become their strength as they fight the negative thoughts inside their own mind. If they overcome them, they finally reach inner peace. The message behind the game is simple: don't spend your life counting down the days—remember what really counts.

Artwork - Lan designed and illustrated our main character, hand-painting the textures directly onto the model to give it its own personality. We also created many of our own textures throughout the game and hand-drew the memory postcards to tell the character's story through moments from their life.

Audio - Alex created the music by taking inspiration from **The Final Countdown** and blending it with the sound of a heart-rate monitor. As the player progresses, the music builds tension before becoming calmer as they finally reach inner peace.
